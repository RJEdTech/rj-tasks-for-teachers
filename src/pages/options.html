My Classes card

The card in Canvas’s right sidebar and the tabs in the full view follow this order. Uncheck a section to leave it off the card; it stays in the full view.

    Check-up

    Turn off any check you don’t want. You can also click Ignore on a single item.

    Students to check on

    List students with at least this many missing assignments this grading period.

    Classes

    Uncheck a class to leave it out too.

    Dark mode
    Where it shows up

    The My Classes card sits in Canvas’s right sidebar on your Dashboard (all your classes) and on each class’s home page (that class).

    Help and questions

    Canvas questions? Start with the Regis Jesuit Professional Development course in Canvas.

    Something not working, or an idea for RJ Tasks for Teachers? Use the feedback form (RJ sign-in required). A screenshot helps with bugs.

    RJ Tasks for Teachers reads your classes from Canvas in your browser and never changes anything there. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Privacy and help
